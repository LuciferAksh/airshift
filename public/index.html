      How the plan works

      Forecast, with context

      Live plans use tomorrow’s regional Open-Meteo / CAMS forecast for the selected location. The model grid in India is roughly 45 km and can miss local spikes. Compare against official readings and follow your local advisories.

      Open-Meteo · CAMS · CPCB Indian AQI

      A comparison you can check

      Each outdoor minute contributes its hourly forecast concentration. The planner keeps the fixed commitment and compares feasible times for the same flexible activity. The resulting concentration × time compares planned outdoor exposure. Indoor conditions, personal dose, and health outcomes require different information.

      AirNow activity guidance

      Travel and indoor air

      Car kilometres count only the solo distance you enter as avoided. Outdoor walking and transfers need a separate check. Indoor actions are based on your available options and explain which particle source or pathway they affect.
